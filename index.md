# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

JAT DoubleD is an audio processing plugin for guitar.

**Data Collection**
JAT DoubleD does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. 
The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, 
and does not share any information with third parties.

**Audio**
Audio processing occurs entirely on your device. No audio data is 
stored or transmitted.
